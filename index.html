Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some of our FAQs. If you have any other questions you’d like answered please feel free to email us.

What is Bookmark?

Bookmark is a browser extension that helps you store, organize, search, and share your favourite websites across devices.

How can I request a new browser?

Contact us with the browser you want supported. We prioritize requests based on demand, compatibility, and platform stability.

Is there a mobile app?

Not yet. Mobile support is planned, and bookmarks can still be accessed from supported browser environments.

What about other Chromium browsers?

Support for additional Chromium-based browsers is being evaluated. You can request your preferred browser through the contact form.
